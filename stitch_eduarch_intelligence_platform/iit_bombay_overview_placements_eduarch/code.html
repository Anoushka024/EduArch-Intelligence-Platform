Institution Overview

Established in 1958, IIT Bombay is the second-oldest institute of the Indian Institutes of Technology system and is a worldwide leader in the field of engineering education and research. The institute is known for its strong faculty, who are among the best in the country, and for its gifted students, who are chosen through the highly competitive Joint Entrance Examination.
